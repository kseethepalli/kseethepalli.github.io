Personal application

This is a private, personal application. It is used only by its owner, on his own computer, to read his own email and to send reports to himself and his household. It is not a product or a service and is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

  • Who can use it: only the owner, with his own Google accounts.
  • What it accesses: the owner's own Gmail messages (read-only) and the ability to create and send email from the owner's own account.
  • What it does with that data: it is processed on the owner's own computer. Nothing is collected from anyone else, nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with third parties.
  • Storage: data stays on the owner's own devices. There is no server, no database and no analytics.
  • Google user data: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Removing access: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the owner's GitHub profile, github.com/kseethepalli.
